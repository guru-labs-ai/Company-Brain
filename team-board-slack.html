What the team does

Three things. Everything else is the bot's job.

Add a task

Add a row to the board, tap "New task" in the channel, or turn any Slack message into a task with "Add to list". Your asks in Slack become tasks the same way.

Work on it

Set it to Doing. If it gets stuck, set it to Blocked and pick who it's waiting on. Watchers get told about changes.

Mark it Done

That's the last click. The bot records the day, adds it to Friday's summary and, for repeating tasks, adds the next one.

The board

This week on top, past weeks underneath.

Every task belongs to a work week. Each Monday the bot opens the new week and moves anything unfinished into it. Finished tasks stay in the week they were done, so the board doubles as a record of what shipped when.

How a week runs

The bot keeps the rhythm, on your time.

All times are Cancun time. It runs on its own from GitHub, the same way the company reports do, and posts a warning in the channel if anything ever fails.

Mon9:00

New week. Unfinished tasks roll forward, carry-overs are counted, and anything stuck for 3 weeks is called out.

Tue to Fri9:00

Deadlines. Overdue, blocked, due today and tomorrow, owners tagged. Silent when nothing is due.

All dayevery 3 hours

Quiet upkeep. Done dates, repeating tasks, new tasks put in the right week. No messages.

Fri16:00

Shipped this week. Everything finished, grouped by product. The one post worth reading.

Files and documents

Everything ends up in the company Google account.

Slack is where we work. The company Google Drive is where things are kept.

Anything uploaded to Slack, and every doc that lives in ClickUp today, gets stored in the company Google account. Tasks on the board carry the link, so nothing lives only in a chat or only in one person's account.

Day to day
Screenshots can sit on the task. Documents go in the company Drive and the task holds the link.
From ClickUp
All ClickUp docs move to the company Drive before ClickUp is cancelled: the active ones into product folders, the rest into an archive folder.
One owner
The company account owns the Drive and shares the top folder with the team once, so every link just opens.
Optional

Two extras, only if you want them.

A weekly sprint review people write themselves

Every Friday the bot creates a Slack doc with a section per person, using your five questions, with what the board already knows shown under each one. Each person writes their own answers, and the Friday post lists who hasn't yet.

What shipped this week?What ships next week?What's blocked, and who's needed?What's late?What metric moved?

A revenue line in the Friday post

One line above "Shipped this week" with the week's revenue per product, straight from Stripe, so the team sees the work next to the money. Your call whether the whole team should see it.
